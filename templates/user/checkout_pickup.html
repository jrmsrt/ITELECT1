How Pickup Works

  1. Place your order and select Pickup as the delivery method.
  2. Wait for a confirmation email or notification that your order is ready for pickup.
  3. Visit the BookHaven store within store hours.
  4. Present your order confirmation (digital or printed).
  5. Collect your books and enjoy reading! 📚

⏳ Orders are usually ready within 1–2 business days. Please pick up your order within 7 days after confirmation.
